The Lantau Trail was opened in 1984 as a long-distance route round Lantau, the largest island in Hong Kong. It starts and ends at Mui Wo, the ferry town on Silvermine Bay, and stays almost entirely within the Lantau North and Lantau South country parks.

The first stages climb straight onto the island's mountain spine, over Sunset Peak and Lantau Peak, at 934 m the second-highest summit in Hong Kong, before dropping to the monastery plateau at Ngong Ping. The trail then turns west to the fishing village of Tai O and the remote south-west tip, and returns along the southern coast past reservoirs and long beaches.

The Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department describes a 70 km circular route divided into twelve stages. There are campsites along the way, and frequent bus links make it easy to walk the stages one at a time.
