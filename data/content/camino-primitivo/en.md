---
title: "Camino Primitivo"
summary: "The oldest Jacobean route, said to follow the journey of King Alfonso II in the ninth century, leads from Oviedo across the Cantabrian mountains to Santiago."
status: reviewed
---

The Primitivo is a short, hilly road through Asturias and Galicia, crossing the mountain pass at the Hospitales route and joining the Camino Francés at Melide for the last days. It is quieter than the Francés and has steep climbs.

It is usually walked in about 14 stages.
