---
title: "Long Trail"
summary: "America's oldest long-distance hiking trail follows the Green Mountains for 272 miles from the Massachusetts border to the Canadian border."
status: reviewed
---

The Long Trail was built in the early twentieth century by the Green Mountain Club and later inspired the Appalachian Trail, with which it shares the first 100 miles. North of the split it gets steeper and rougher, crossing Vermont's highest summits, including Mount Mansfield and Camel's Hump, on rocky, often muddy paths with simple shelters along the way.

A thru-hike takes a few weeks. Most hikers go north in summer or early autumn, and the club asks walkers to stay off the high trails during the spring mud season, when the soil is easily damaged.
