---
title: "Long Trail"
summary: "America's oldest long-distance hiking trail follows the Green Mountains for 272 miles from the Massachusetts border to the Canadian border."
status: reviewed
---

The Long Trail was built in the early twentieth century by the Green Mountain Club and later inspired the Appalachian Trail, with which it shares the first 100 miles.

The club says the trail runs 272 miles across the Vermont ridge and that a thru-hike takes a few weeks.
