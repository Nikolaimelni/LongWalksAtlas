---
title: "Hong Kong Trail"
summary: "A 50 km trail across Hong Kong Island from Victoria Peak to Big Wave Bay, opened in 1985, in 8 sections."
status: machine
---

The Hong Kong Trail is one of the four long-distance trails managed by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department and crosses five country parks. It is split into 8 sections.

The eight sections can be joined into one long day or shorter walks, and the trail starts at the best-known viewpoint in the city. It was opened in April 1985.
