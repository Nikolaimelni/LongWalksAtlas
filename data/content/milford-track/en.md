---
title: "Milford Track"
summary: "New Zealand's most famous Great Walk crosses Fiordland over four days from Glade Wharf on Lake Te Anau, over the McKinnon Pass, to Sandfly Point on Milford Sound."
status: reviewed
---

The Milford Track follows the Clinton and Arthur valleys, climbs over the Mackinnon Pass and descends along the Arthur River past Sutherland Falls, one of the highest waterfalls in New Zealand. Walkers have to book huts in advance and walk in one direction only.

The Great Walks season runs from 1 November to 30 April.
