---
title: "Juliana Trail"
summary: "A circular long-distance trail around the Julian Alps in Slovenia, 16 stages that skirt the main summits and follow the Soča and Sava rivers."
status: machine
---

The Juliana Trail connects valleys, passes, forests and villages around Triglav National Park without requiring summit climbs. It is signposted and described in 16 stages of about 17.5 km each.

Two optional extensions, of 42 km and 60 km, branch off the main circle. The season runs from late March to late October.
