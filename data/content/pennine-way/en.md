---
title: "Pennine Way"
summary: "England's first National Trail runs along the Pennine hills from Edale in the Peak District to Kirk Yetholm just across the Scottish border."
status: reviewed
---

The route crosses gritstone moorland, limestone dales and the high fells of the Yorkshire Dales and North Pennines, then follows Hadrian's Wall before climbing into the Cheviot Hills. Peat bog and exposed ridges make it a navigation challenge in poor weather.
