The PCT runs through the Sierra Nevada and the Cascade Range, crossing deserts, high passes, snow and volcanic landscapes. Its length is given by the Pacific Crest Trail Association as 2,650 miles.

Thru-hikes usually take about five months, and snow and fire conditions dominate planning.
