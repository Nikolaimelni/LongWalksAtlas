title: "Kilimanjaro - Machame Route"
summary: "One of the main routes up Mount Kilimanjaro, starting at Machame Gate and using tented camps."
status: machine
---

The Machame route is one of the routes on Kilimanjaro and uses tented camps run by licensed operators. Tanzania National Parks names Machame as one of the main gates, with descent via Mweka.

Tanzania National Parks notes that the Marangu route uses huts while the other routes, including Machame, use tented camps. Descent is by the Mweka gate.
