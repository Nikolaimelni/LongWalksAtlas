---
title: "Rennsteig"
summary: "A ridge trail through the Thuringian Forest from Hörschel on the Werra to Blankenstein on the Saale, long used as a boundary path."
status: machine
---

The Rennsteig follows the crest of the Thuringian Forest and Thuringian Slate Mountains and is one of Germany's oldest and best-known hiking routes. It is signposted with a white R on a green ground.

Walkers usually split it into four, six or eight stages.
