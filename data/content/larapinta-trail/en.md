---
title: "Larapinta Trail"
summary: "A desert trail in the West MacDonnell Ranges of central Australia, from Alice Springs Telegraph Station to the summit of Mount Sonder, in twelve sections."
status: machine
---

Larapinta follows the ridges of the West MacDonnell Ranges through gorges and waterholes. The government page says it is over 230 km and divided into 12 sections of one to two days each.

Other sources quote 223 km; the trail is usually walked in cooler months.
