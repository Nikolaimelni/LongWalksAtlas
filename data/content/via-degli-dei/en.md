title: "Via degli Dei"
summary: "A path over the Apennines from Bologna to Florence that follows partly Roman and medieval roads and is usually walked in five to seven days."
status: machine
---

The Via degli Dei links the two cities across forested ridges, old roads and mountain villages, with overnight stops in guesthouses. The name refers to mountain place names with ancient gods, such as Monte Adone and Monte Venere.
