---
title: "Jesus Trail"
summary: "A pilgrim and hiking route of about 65 km in Galilee from Nazareth to Capernaum on the Sea of Galilee."
status: reviewed
---

The Jesus Trail follows paths linked to Jesus' ministry through Cana, the Horns of Hattin and Mount Arbel. It is a mix of Christian pilgrimage and nature walk, and is usually completed in three to four days.

The trail website gives 65 km.
