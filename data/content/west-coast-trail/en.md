---
title: "West Coast Trail"
summary: "A rugged coastal trail on the west coast of Vancouver Island, 75 km of beaches, ladders and rainforest between Pachena Bay and Gordon River."
status: machine
---

The route was originally a lifesaving trail for shipwreck survivors and is now a strenuous hike in the Pacific Rim National Park Reserve. Hikers must reserve and attend an orientation, and the trail is only open in summer.
