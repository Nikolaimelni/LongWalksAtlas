---
title: "Te Araroa"
summary: "New Zealand's national trail runs about 3,000 km from Cape Reinga in the far north to Bluff at the southern tip."
status: machine
---

The trail crosses beaches, forests, volcanic landscapes and mountain ranges across both islands and uses a mix of tracks, roads and river and sea crossings.

The trail trust gives 3,000 km as the full length. A thru-hike takes months.
