title: "John Muir Trail"
summary: "A high-altitude Sierra Nevada trail of about 211 miles from Yosemite Valley over passes above 3,500 m to the summit of Mount Whitney."
status: machine
---

The John Muir Trail follows the crest of the Sierra Nevada through Yosemite, Kings Canyon and Sequoia National Parks. Every hiker needs a wilderness permit.

Most hikers need two to three weeks and carry food for long stretches without resupply.
