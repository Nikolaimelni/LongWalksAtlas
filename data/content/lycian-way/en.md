---
title: Lycian Way
summary: A waymarked footpath along the coast of ancient Lycia in southern Turkey, from Ölüdeniz near Fethiye to Geyikbayırı near Antalya.
status: reviewed
---

Laid out and waymarked in 1999, the path links old mule tracks and Roman roads past the tombs and theatres of Lycian cities, with long stretches high above the sea.
