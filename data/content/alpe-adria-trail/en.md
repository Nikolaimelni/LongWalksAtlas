---
title: "Alpe-Adria-Trail"
summary: "A long-distance trail from the foot of the Grossglockner in Austria through Slovenia's Julian Alps to the Adriatic coast near Trieste."
status: reviewed
---

The Alpe-Adria-Trail crosses the Alps from glaciers to the sea, passing Carinthian lakes, the Slovenian Soča valley and the Karst plateau before dropping to Muggia. It is divided into 43 stages for hiking from village to village.

Several stages have alternative lines, so the total distance depends on which variants you take.
