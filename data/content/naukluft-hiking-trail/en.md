The Naukluft trail is a circular trail in the Naukluft Mountains. Namibia Wildlife Resorts offers 4-day and 8-day versions.

Namibia Wildlife Resorts runs the trail from its Naukluft camp. The longer 8-day version crosses the high plateau, and the shorter version is meant for hikers with less time.
