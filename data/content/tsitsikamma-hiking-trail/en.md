---
title: "Tsitsikamma Hiking Trail"
summary: "A six-day mountain hiking trail through indigenous forest and fynbos in the Tsitsikamma, from Nature's Valley to Storms River with huts at each overnight stop."
status: machine
---

The trail passes river gorges and huge yellowwood trees and can also be walked in shorter versions. Optional porterage is available.

The Eastern Cape tourism site gives 62.3 km over six days.
