---
title: "Hadrian's Wall Path"
summary: "A National Trail along the Roman frontier between Wallsend on the Tyne and Bowness-on-Solway, passing forts, milecastles and the crags of the Whin Sill."
status: reviewed
---

The route crosses northern England coast to coast, mostly along or close to the line of the second-century wall. The central section over the Whin Sill crags offers the best preserved remains, with forts such as Housesteads and Vindolanda a short distance from the path.

The wall is part of a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and walkers are asked to stay off it and to keep away in the wettest months, when the grass over buried remains wears through easily.
