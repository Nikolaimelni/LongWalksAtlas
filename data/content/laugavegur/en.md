---
title: "Laugavegur Trail"
summary: "Iceland's most popular trek crosses the southern highlands in about four days, from the rhyolite hot springs of Landmannalaugar to the green valleys of Þórsmörk."
status: machine
---

The path passes steaming vents, black obsidian fields, snowfields and glacial rivers before ending in a birch-scrub valley between three glaciers. Huts along the way are run by the Icelandic Touring Association, and the trail is only passable in summer.

The huts open from around 25 June to 15 September.
