---
title: "GR11 Transpirenaica"
summary: "The Spanish side of the Pyrenean traverse, a long mountain footpath from Cabo de Higuer on the Atlantic to Cap de Creus on the Mediterranean."
status: reviewed
---

The GR11 follows the southern slopes of the Pyrenees through the Basque Country, Navarre, Aragon and Catalonia, crossing the main range several times and staying close to the French border. It is split into 46 stages by the Spanish mountaineering federation, plus a prologue.

It is commonly walked in sections; a full traverse takes well over a month because of the steady climbs between valleys.
