---
title: "Kerry Way"
summary: "A loop around the Iveragh Peninsula in County Kerry, starting and ending in Killarney, through mountains, bog and coastal villages such as Cahersiveen and Sneem."
status: machine
---

The Kerry Way combines quiet roads, old tracks and mountain paths between the Macgillycuddy's Reeks and the Atlantic coast. About a third of the route is on local roads.
