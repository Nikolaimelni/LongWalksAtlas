---
title: "Shikoku Pilgrimage"
summary: "A circular pilgrimage of 88 temples around the island of Shikoku, about 1,200 km, associated with the monk Kūkai."
status: reviewed
---

The Shikoku Henro is the best-known circular pilgrimage in Japan, walked clockwise starting from temple 1 at Ryōzen-ji. Many pilgrims walk it in sections.

Because the circuit passes the island's whole coast and mountains, many pilgrims do it in several trips. White pilgrim clothing and a staff are traditional, but they are not required for walkers today.
