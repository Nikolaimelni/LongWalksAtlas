---
title: E1 European long-distance path
summary: The north–south European long-distance path, from Nordkapp in Norway through Scandinavia, Denmark, Germany and Switzerland to Capo Passero in Sicily.
status: machine
---

The E1 strings together national and regional trails under one waymark. Its southern end was extended through Italy to Sicily in the 2010s, and parts of the Italian section are still being signed.
