---
title: "Jordan Trail"
summary: "A 675 km trail from Umm Qais in the north of Jordan through Dana and Petra to Aqaba on the Red Sea, typically walked in about 35 days."
status: reviewed
---

The Jordan Trail crosses Jordan from the Roman ruins at Umm Qais over the Jordan Rift mountains, the Dead Sea highlands, Dana, Petra and Wadi Rum to the Red Sea. It connects local villages with Bedouin communities.
