title: "Alta Via 1"
summary: "The classic high route through the Dolomites from the Lago di Braies to Belluno, with huts every day and twelve stages."
status: machine
---

Alta Via 1 links the Pragser Wildsee with the Cinque Torri, the Civetta group and the Piave valley, crossing high passes and rock walls that are now UNESCO listed. Its stage plan is designed around mountain huts, which open from late June to late September.
