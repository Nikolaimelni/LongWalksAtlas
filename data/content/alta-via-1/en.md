---
title: "Alta Via 1"
summary: "The classic high route through the Dolomites from the Lago di Braies to Belluno, with huts every day and twelve stages."
status: reviewed
---

Alta Via 1 crosses the Dolomites from north to south, from the Pragser Wildsee, or Lago di Braies, in South Tyrol to the city of Belluno. It links the Fanes plateau with the Cinque Torri, the Civetta group and the Piave valley, crossing high passes and rock walls that were listed by UNESCO in 2009.

The first days lead through the Fanes-Sennes-Braies nature park, high limestone plateaus with pastures and small lakes. The route then climbs to the Lagazuoi, at about 2,750 m the highest point of the walk, where tunnels and trenches from the First World War cut through the mountain. Beyond the Falzarego pass come the Cinque Torri and the Nuvolau, then the long traverse below the north-west face of the Civetta, with the Pelmo across the valley. The last section crosses the Schiara group, where walkers choose between a via ferrata and an easier path down to the valley and Belluno.

Alta Via 1 is considered the easiest of the Dolomite high routes. It needs no climbing on the main line, though some paths are steep, exposed or protected by cables. Days are mostly five to seven hours.

Its stage plan is designed around mountain huts, which open from late June to late September. They serve hot meals and offer dormitory or private rooms, and in July and August they need booking well ahead. Several huts can be reached by cable car or by road, which makes it easy to leave the route or skip a stage. Buses run from Dobbiaco and Cortina to Lago di Braies, and Belluno has trains and buses back north.

Snow can lie on north-facing slopes into early July. Summer thunderstorms are common in the afternoon, and the walk is best started early each day.
