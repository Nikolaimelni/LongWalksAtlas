title: "Heysen Trail"
summary: "South Australia's long walk runs about 1,200 km from Cape Jervis through the Fleurieu Peninsula, the Barossa and the Flinders Ranges to Parachilna Gorge."
status: machine
---

The Heysen Trail is built and looked after by the Friends of the Heysen Trail and crosses farmland, vineyards and the Flinders Ranges. It is closed during the fire danger season.
