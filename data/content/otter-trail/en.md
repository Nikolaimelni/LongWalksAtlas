title: "Otter Trail"
summary: "South Africa's first and most famous slackpacking-style hiking trail follows the Tsitsikamma coast for 45 km between Storms River Mouth and Nature's Valley in five days."
status: machine
---

The Otter Trail is a booked-only coastal trail in Garden Route National Park with huts at the overnight stops and river crossings that depend on tides.

SANParks describes it as 45 km over five days and four nights.
