---
title: "Kungsleden"
summary: "Sweden's famous Arctic hut-to-hut trail runs about 450 km from Abisko above the Arctic Circle to Hemavan in the south, through Lapland's mountains and valleys."
status: reviewed
---

Kungsleden is run by the Swedish Tourist Association, which maintains huts, bridges and boat transfers. Its best-known section is the north, from Abisko through Tjäktja pass to Nikkaluokta, where walkers cross open tundra and alpine valleys beneath Sweden's highest mountains.

The exact distance depends on which side trips you add and how many of the lake crossings you make by boat.
