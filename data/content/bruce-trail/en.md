---
title: "Bruce Trail"
summary: "Canada's oldest and longest marked footpath follows the Niagara Escarpment for more than 900 km from Niagara to Tobermory on the Bruce Peninsula."
status: reviewed
---

The Bruce Trail Conservancy describes a main trail of more than 900 km plus about 400 km of side trails, which together make up 1,300 km. It passes cliffs, caves, forests and small towns between Lake Ontario and Georgian Bay.
