---
title: "Peaks of the Balkans Trail"
summary: "A cross-border circuit through the Accursed Mountains of Albania, Kosovo and Montenegro, linking guesthouse villages such as Theth, Valbona, Plav and Peja."
status: reviewed
---

The trail was created after the wars of the 1990s as a joint project to encourage cooperation between three countries. It uses shepherd paths, passes above 2,000 m and stays in village guesthouses, so walkers need no tent.

It is a loop of ten stages, usually started in Theth, Plav or Peja.
