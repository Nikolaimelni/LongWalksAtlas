---
title: "Transcaucasian Trail: Upper Svaneti"
summary: "A 140 km high-mountain section of the Transcaucasian Trail through Georgia's Svaneti, from Chuberi in the Nenskra valley to Ushguli, one of Europe's highest villages."
status: reviewed
---

The Upper Svaneti hike goes through villages with stone defence towers, over passes and past glaciers of the Greater Caucasus. It is run by the Transcaucasian Trail Association, a non-profit that is building a continuous trail across the Caucasus.

From the Nenskra valley the route crosses forested passes to Mestia, the main town of the region, then follows the well-known trek through Zhabeshi, Adishi and Iprali to Ushguli, with the twin peaks of Ushba and the long wall of Shkhara, Georgia's highest summit, in view. Upper Svaneti is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and walkers sleep in family guesthouses in the villages.
