---
title: "Carian Trail"
summary: "A long trail through ancient Caria in south-west Turkey, from the coast near Marmaris to the inland ruins around Alinda."
status: reviewed
---

The Carian Trail links the Bozburun and Datça peninsulas, Gökova Bay and inner Caria and passes ancient ruins, old caravan roads and mule paths.

The trail's own website gives about 850 km in four main sections; the Culture Routes society states 800 km.
