---
title: "Nakasendō"
summary: "The old highway between Kyoto and Edo (Tokyo), about 534 km with 69 post towns, partly preserved as a walking trail at Magome and Tsumago."
status: machine
---

The Nakasendo was one of the five roads of the Edo period and connected Kyoto and Edo through the mountains. Sections such as the Magome-Tsumago path in the Kiso valley are well preserved.

In the Edo period there were 69 post towns along the road.
