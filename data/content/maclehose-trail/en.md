---
title: "MacLehose Trail"
summary: "Hong Kong's longest hiking trail, about 100 km in 10 sections from East Dam in Sai Kung to Tuen Mun."
status: reviewed
---

The MacLehose Trail crosses the New Territories through country parks, beaches and mountains, passing the Sai Kung coast, Sha Tin and Tai Mo Shan. It is a government-managed trail and the Oxfam Trailwalker route.

The trail is split into 10 sections, which can be walked one at a time.
