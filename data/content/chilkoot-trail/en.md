---
title: "Chilkoot Trail"
summary: "A historic gold rush route from Dyea, Alaska over the Chilkoot Pass to Lake Bennett in British Columbia, 33 miles long."
status: machine
---

In 1897-98 prospectors carried tons of supplies over the pass on their way to the Klondike. Today the trail is managed jointly by the US National Park Service and Parks Canada and is open in summer.

The NPS gives 33 miles.
