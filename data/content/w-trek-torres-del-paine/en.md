---
title: "Torres del Paine W Trek"
summary: "A five-day trek in Patagonia's Torres del Paine National Park along three valleys shaped like a W: Grey Glacier, the French Valley and the base of the Towers."
status: reviewed
---

The W trek links the Torres, Valle Francés and Grey Glacier, with refugios and campsites in the park.

Chile's tourism board gives about 70 km and five days and calls spring and summer the walking season.
